Blog

Articles on Arabic NLP, efficient AI models, semantic search, and machine learning engineering. Deep dives into building production AI systems by Abdelkareem Elkhateb.

Welcome to my blog! Here, I share deep dives, tutorials, and insights into the world of Arabic Natural Language Processing (NLP), Machine Learning Engineering, and building efficient AI systems.

My writing focuses on practical applications, from optimizing transformer models for production to exploring the latest advancements in semantic search and vector databases. Whether you are an AI researcher, a software engineer, or a tech enthusiast, you’ll find articles aimed at making complex AI concepts accessible and actionable.

The Philosophy Behind My Writing

In the rapidly evolving field of Artificial Intelligence, the gap between academic research and production-grade engineering can often feel vast. My goal with this blog is to bridge that gap by sharing hands-on experiences, architectural patterns, and performance optimizations.

I believe that for AI to be truly useful, it must be accessible, efficient, and well-understood. This is especially true for the Arabic language, which presents unique linguistic and technical challenges. Through these posts, I aim to provide a resource for fellow engineers and researchers who are passionate about building the next generation of intelligent systems.

By documenting my journey, I hope to contribute to a more robust AI ecosystem. If you are interested in my academic background, you can explore my Research Papers. For a look at the tools and libraries I build, visit my Open Source Projects. I also maintain a Today I Learned section for smaller, daily technical discoveries.
