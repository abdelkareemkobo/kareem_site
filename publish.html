مشاريع الكتابة الطويلة

هذا القسم مخصص لكتبي القادمة وأدلّتي التقنية الطويلة. أعمل حالياً على عدة مشاريع تهدف إلى تقديم معرفة عميقة ومنظّمة للمهندسين والباحثين في مجال الذكاء الاصطناعي. هدفي هو تجميع سنوات من الخبرة في هندسة التعلم الآلي و NLP العربية في موارد عالية الجودة وسهلة المنال.

Why Long-form Content?

While my blog and Today I Learned notes are great for quick insights, some topics require a more comprehensive and structured approach. Books allow for a deep dive into architectural patterns, theoretical foundations, and end-to-end implementation details that are often too complex for a single article.

Current Projects in Progress

  • The Arabic NLP Handbook: This book aims to be a comprehensive guide to building modern NLP systems specifically tailored for the Arabic language. It covers unique linguistic challenges, from classical morphological analysis to fine-tuning state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) on Arabic datasets.
  • Production AI Infrastructure: Drawing from my experience as a Staff ML Engineer, this project focuses on the practicalities of building and scaling machine learning systems in production. It covers MLOps, CI/CD for data pipelines, and robust monitoring strategies.
  • Efficient Transformers: A deep dive into making AI models faster and smaller. We explore techniques like model distillation, quantization, and architectural pruning to enable the deployment of sophisticated AI on edge devices and in cost-constrained environments.
